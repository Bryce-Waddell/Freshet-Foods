  • Increased risk of spoilage & loss of flavor
  • Exports jobs and value
  • Undercuts domestic dockside economies

Freshet Foods is building the processing infrastructure to reverse this cycle.

Our Mission

To process, preserve, and distribute the finest domestic harvests. We exist to revitalize coastal communities, restore our native ecosystems, and prove that the highest quality food doesn't need to cross an ocean.
  • 01
    Revitalize coastal communities

    Processing on the waterfront keeps jobs, wages, and dockside business in the towns that land the catch.

  • 02
    Restore native ecosystems

    Building markets for the species and crops our waters and soils need harvested, turning ecological pressure into a product line.

  • 03
    Shorten the supply chain

    A short, direct route from harvest to freezer. Fewer miles, fewer hands, fewer places for quality to slip.
